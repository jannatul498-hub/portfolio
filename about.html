My Story

My name is Jannatul Ferdaus, and I am a student of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering (ETE) at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), one of Bangladesh's most prestigious public universities. I earned my admission in 2023 after achieving a perfect GPA of 5.00 in both my SSC from Uttara High School and College and my HSC examinations — a testament to my dedication and love for learning.

I grew up with a deep curiosity about how things work — from the circuits in a radio to the roots of a plant in a garden. This dual love for engineering and nature has shaped my personality in unique ways. I find the same patience required to grow a seedling applies beautifully to debugging a circuit or iterating on a robotics design.

Beyond academics, I am passionate about photography — particularly capturing the quiet beauty of gardens and the vibrant energy of engineering labs. I am also an avid storyteller and reciter; I believe language and narrative are just as powerful as technology in changing the world. My ability to communicate fluently in English allows me to engage with global ideas and perspectives.

In my second semester at RUET, I built a Dementia Patient Assistive Device, a project that blended embedded systems, human-centered design, and compassion for vulnerable communities. This experience solidified my belief that engineering's highest purpose is serving people. More recently, I have been working on a Departmental Staff Monitoring, Resource Management, and Complaint Management System, a web-based project designed to improve staff attendance monitoring, departmental resource management, and complaint handling. Through this project, I am gaining practical experience in full-stack development, database management, and designing technology-driven solutions for real-world institutional needs. I am currently deepening my knowledge in robotics, programming, and signal processing as I progress through my degree — always curious, always growing.

Career Goals

Graduate from RUET with excellence in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering and contribute meaningfully to research in assistive technology.
Develop innovative robotics and embedded systems solutions that improve quality of life for people with disabilities and the elderly.
Pursue advanced studies or research opportunities — domestically or internationally — in the fields of human-robot interaction or biomedical engineering.
Build a career that bridges technology and creativity, combining engineering precision with storytelling and design thinking.
Inspire other young women in Bangladesh to pursue STEM fields with confidence and passion.
