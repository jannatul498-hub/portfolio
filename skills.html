Learning Philosophy

I believe that skills are not destinations — they are ever-expanding horizons. Every semester at RUET introduces new challenges in signal theory, circuit design, and programming that push me to grow. Outside the classroom, I self-study topics like robotics, photography composition, and creative writing. I treat every project — whether it's building an assistive device or photographing a garden flower at golden hour — as a learning opportunity.

My approach: curiosity first, consistency second, excellence always.
